# Engineering Services — English Content

## Page introduction

**Engineering Services**

Practical engineering support for robotics, embedded systems, intelligent prototypes, and industrial control. Engagements are defined around a clear technical scope, agreed deliverables, and documentation that makes the work reproducible.

**Every engagement begins with a brief:** share the objective, current technical constraints, available hardware or data, and the expected deliverable. The scope is then confirmed before work begins.

## ROS, SLAM & Robot Simulation

### ROS / ROS 2 Robotics Simulation & Development

Develop ROS or ROS 2 robot simulations in Gazebo or Webots, including URDF models, virtual-sensor integration, Python or C++ behavior nodes, and custom test environments. Delivery can include source code, setup instructions, and reproducible run commands.

### UAV Simulation in ROS–Gazebo

Integrate a UAV model into a realistic simulation environment and evaluate multiple flight scenarios before physical testing. The deliverable can include the simulation workspace, configuration files, scenario definitions, and a concise test summary.

### CAD-to-URDF Robot Models

Convert SolidWorks CAD assemblies into simulation-ready URDF packages. The work can include Gazebo components, collision geometry, joint definitions, and inertial-property tuning for Gazebo, Webots, or CoppeliaSim.

### AI-Assisted Navigation & Obstacle Avoidance

Develop and integrate prototype AI or machine-learning modules that support robot autonomy, navigation, and obstacle avoidance in ROS-based systems. Delivery focuses on integration-ready software components and documented evaluation scenarios.

## Embedded Systems & IoT

### Embedded Firmware for Connected Devices

Develop firmware for STM32, ESP32, Arduino, or Raspberry Pi projects, including Wi-Fi, BLE, or MQTT communication. Delivery includes documented source code, connection guidance, and build or upload instructions.

### PCB Design & Communication Interfaces

Create PCB designs in Altium, KiCad, or EasyEDA and prepare manufacturing-ready Gerber files. Support can also cover driver development and reusable interface libraries for I²C, SPI, CAN, Modbus, or LoRa.

## Mechanical Design & CAD

### 3D CAD & Machine-Part Design

Model precise mechanical parts, enclosures, and machine components in SolidWorks or Fusion 360 from client drawings or requirements. Deliverables can include editable CAD files and STEP exports prepared for prototyping or manufacture.

## Engineering Consulting & Technical Documentation

### Mechatronics Design Review & Technical Consulting

Review system concepts, technical drawings, and prototype architectures for robotics and mechatronics projects. Support is available as a written recommendation report or a remote technical consultation.

### Robotics Research & Technical Review

Provide structured technical and methodological review for robotics and autonomous-systems research materials, including clarity of system design, experimental setup, and technical documentation. This service supports quality review; it does not replace authorship or academic work.

## Artificial Intelligence & Computer Vision

### Custom Object Detection & Vision Pipelines

Train custom object-detection models using YOLO and build OpenCV image or video-processing pipelines around client data. Deliverables may include trained weights, evaluation results, and an integration-ready code package.

### On-Device Local LLM Integration

Prepare compact local language-model deployments that operate without an internet connection on suitable embedded or edge hardware. Delivery can include an environment setup, deployment package, and runtime instructions tailored to the target device.

## Control, Simulation & Industrial Systems

### Control-System Modelling, Simulation & PLC Logic

Model and evaluate control systems in MATLAB/Simulink, including PID loops and robotic-arm kinematic studies. Support can also include logical design and test-oriented review of PLC control programs, with simulation files and a digital results report.

### End-to-End IoT Applications

Develop integrated IoT prototypes from sensor acquisition to a cloud-connected dashboard. The delivery is defined as a deployable software package with device-side code, a documented data path, and deployment guidance.
