## Verified service patterns emerging

1. Scoped technical review or discovery report.
2. Requirements and functional analysis.
3. Architecture, CAD, electronics, software, or firmware design deliverables.
4. Remote programming, simulation, configuration, and technical writing.
5. Test planning, validation review, and data/log analysis.
6. Proof-of-concept definition and iterative development advisory.
7. Project management, documentation, and commercialization or technical strategy advisory.
8. Open-source infrastructure, CI, maintenance strategy, and upstream-contribution support.
9. Commissioned research, technology-landscape studies, community surveys, and roadmap recommendations.
10. Remote workshops, technical talks, and specialist training.
11. Remote robotics advisory focused on a specific robotics stack or autonomous-system ecosystem.
12. Paid technical learning material, guided projects, or cohort-style training built from engineering expertise.
13. Hardware/firmware architecture review, board bring-up support, driver/bootloader work, and remote fault diagnosis.
14. Technology-niche advisory positioned around a specific product class, protocol, stack, or engineering domain.
