# Industrial Symbol Comparison Review

## Reference characteristics adopted

The supplied references rely on recognisable mechanical and electronic forms: a multi-joint industrial robotic arm, a gear paired with a microchip, and circuitry with a restrained monoline treatment. They do not use decorative circles, abstract polygons, or photographic fills.

## Final implementation review

The final site follows those characteristics without copying the supplied art. The Education and Projects sections use a detailed industrial robotic-arm icon. Publications and Skills use a microchip-board icon. Experience uses a paired standard gear and microchip motif with connecting trace lines. All motifs use a single pale-teal technical line treatment, sit beneath the content layer, and are placed asymmetrically at the section edge.

The comparison check was completed at desktop and mobile widths. The former circular/square frames and photographic backgrounds are absent. The motif system now communicates robotics, automation, and embedded electronics directly, while preserving the calm academic layout and leaving all headings, descriptions, records, and project cards unobstructed.

The immediate post-checkpoint public-domain request initially returned the preceding icon release. A cache-busting public recheck after deployment propagation confirmed the new industrial icon composition on the public domain, including the larger robotic-arm mark and the gear-and-chip pairing.
