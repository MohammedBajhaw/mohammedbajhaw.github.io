Autonomous Medical Search & Rescue Robot

A multi-sensor rescue platform powered by Jetson TX2 that combines thermal and RGB vision, bio-radar, LoRa, and local voice guidance.

Search & RescueSensor FusionEdge AILoRa

Overview

An autonomous search-and-rescue platform designed to identify signs of life and hazardous conditions in disaster sites. It fuses thermal and RGB cameras with bio-radar and uses LoRa for long-range communication.

The design includes Cognitive Override and Wake-on-Suspect mechanisms to reduce power consumption, alongside a local LLM-assisted voice interface for initial guidance before rescue teams arrive.

System stack

  • NVIDIA Jetson TX2
  • Thermal + RGB Cameras
  • Bio-Radar
  • LoRa
  • Local LLM / TTS

Outcomes

  • Designed a multi-sensor survivor-search architecture.
  • Integrated preliminary health alerts with a local voice interface.
  • Improved power consumption through Wake-on-Suspect logic.
