RKO-LIO Indoor 3D Mapping Platform

An end-to-end ROS 2 platform that fuses Velodyne LiDAR and Pixhawk IMU data for resilient indoor 3D mapping, then benchmarks robustness against alternative odometry pipelines.

Overview

This project implements and evaluates an indoor 3D mapping platform for environments where GPS is unavailable. The real-robot stack integrates a Velodyne VLP-16 LiDAR, Pixhawk Cube Orange+ IMU, TurtleBot Waffle Pi base, and ROS 2 Humble data flow to generate consistent spatial maps from LiDAR–inertial measurements.

The engineering work focused on deploying RKO-LIO, a sensor-agnostic LiDAR–inertial odometry pipeline built around a kinematic motion model and adaptive scan-to-map regularisation. The system was tested against roll-meter measurements across five indoor objects and benchmarked by replaying the same sensor bag through RKO-LIO, LIO-SAM, and FAST-LIO inside three Gazebo worlds.

The final evaluation demonstrates centimetre-level geometric fidelity on the real platform, while the simulation study exposes the practical reliability trade-off between peak trajectory accuracy and bounded error under difficult mapping conditions. The accompanying IEEE paper documents the experimental design, quantitative results, and full comparison.

System stack

  • ROS 2 Humble
  • RKO-LIO
  • Velodyne VLP-16
  • Pixhawk Cube Orange+
  • TurtleBot Waffle Pi
  • Gazebo
  • rosbag Replay

Outcomes

  • 0.026 m RMSE and 0.022 m MAE against roll-meter ground truth
  • 0.69% mean dimensional error across five indoor test objects
  • Bounded RKO-LIO error across all three Gazebo environments (APE ≤0.494 m)
  • 13.7× improvement over a prior LIO-SAM deployment on identical hardware
