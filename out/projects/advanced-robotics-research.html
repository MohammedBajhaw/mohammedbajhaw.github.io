Advanced Robotics Research

A research platform combining a DJI Matrice 350 RTK, Jetson Orin NX, LiDAR, and RealSense sensors for three-dimensional mapping in GPS-denied environments.

Autonomous SystemsROS 2LiDAR SLAMUAVs

Overview

This project investigates the hardware and software architecture required for aerial robots supporting disaster-management operations. A DJI Matrice 350 RTK platform is integrated with an NVIDIA Jetson Orin NX through the DJI Payload SDK, together with LiDAR and Intel RealSense sensors.

The research focuses on high-accuracy SLAM in GPS-denied environments, progressing from simulation to field experiments. It also contributed to a large-scale victim-detection dataset for model training.

System stack

  • DJI Matrice 350 RTK
  • NVIDIA Jetson Orin NX
  • Velodyne LiDAR
  • Intel RealSense
  • ROS 2

Outcomes

  • High-accuracy 3D mapping in GPS-denied environments.
  • DJI Payload SDK integration with edge computing and multi-sensor payloads.
  • Contributed to an 18,000-image victim-detection dataset.
