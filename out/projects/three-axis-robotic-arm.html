3-Axis Robotic Arm Modeling & Control

Mechanical design and kinematic analysis for a three-axis robotic arm, with MATLAB and Simulink PID control simulation before production.

RoboticsSolidWorksPID ControlMATLAB

Overview

This project brings together mechanical design and control. The structure was designed in SolidWorks, followed by motion modeling and kinematic analysis to validate motion ranges and precision.

Before fabrication, PID control logic was evaluated in MATLAB and Simulink to study response behavior and improve precise motion across all three axes.

System stack

  • SolidWorks
  • MATLAB
  • Simulink
  • PID Control

Outcomes

  • Designed a manufacturable three-axis structure.
  • Completed kinematic analysis for workspace validation.
  • Simulated control behavior before production.
