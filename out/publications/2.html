Demonstrating the Efficiency of RKO-LIO on a DJI UAV Platform Utilizing Drone Built-in IMU for GPS-Denied Mapping

A hardware-software integration framework for GPS-denied UAVs using onboard IMU data and LiDAR SLAM algorithms.

Research Paper2026First author

Abstract

This work evaluates RKO-LIO on a DJI UAV platform and investigates how the built-in IMU can improve the reliability of three-dimensional mapping where GPS signals are unavailable.
